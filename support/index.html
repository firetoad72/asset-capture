Help with Asset Capture on iPhone

Need help or found a bug? Open a support request. Tell us your iPhone model and what happened. Please don't include other people's personal details.

Getting started

  1. Tap New site and enter the site's name. The address and notes are optional.
  2. Tap Add asset. Choose the asset type, add its location and asset ID, and pick its condition and service tag status.
  3. Add a note or photos if you like, then tap Save, or Save, add another to go straight on to the next asset.
  4. Tap any asset in the list to edit it. Use the search box and the Defects and Tag issues filters to find what needs attention.

Photos

On the asset form, tap Add photo to take a photo or choose one from your library. You can add up to 10 photos to each asset. Tap a photo to see it full size, or the × to remove it. Photos are shrunk to save space on your phone.

Condition reports

When the walk is done, tap Report on the site and choose New condition report. Check the date, who inspected and the reference, add an overall summary and any recommended actions, then tap Create report. The report lists every asset as it is at that moment, with its condition, service tag, notes and photos, and calls out the items requiring attention first.

  • Share PDF makes an A4 PDF and opens the share sheet, so you can email it, save it to Files or AirDrop it. Print opens the print dialog.
  • Reports are kept under Report → Past reports. Editing the register later does not change a report; use Update asset list on a report if you do want it to match the register as it is now.
  • Tap the … button on the home screen and choose Your details to have your name, company, licence number and contact details filled in on every report.

Spreadsheet and text

On a site, tap Report and choose Spreadsheet (CSV file) to share the register as a file that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. The … button on the site also offers Copy register as text.

Backing up

On the home screen, tap the … button and choose Back up all data. Asset Capture saves every site, asset, photo and condition report in one file, and opens the share sheet so you can keep it in Files, iCloud Drive or email. On a new phone, choose Restore from a backup and pick that file. Restoring replaces what's on the phone.

Your data

Everything is stored on your phone. There's no account or cloud copy, so save a backup regularly. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the privacy policy.

Important

Asset Capture is a record-keeping tool. It does not inspect, test or certify anything, and its report is not a certificate of compliance or a statutory form.
