Every fire safety asset, captured on site.

Asset Capture is a simple field app for building a site's fire safety asset register. Walk the site, record each extinguisher, hydrant, hose reel, exit light and more with its condition, service tag, notes and photos, then share the register as a PDF report or a spreadsheet. No account, and no signal needed.

Get Asset Capture See what it does

iPhone (App Store). Free.

A clean asset register in minutes

Built for fire safety technicians, building managers and anyone who needs an accurate list of what is on site and what condition it is in.

Sites

Keep a separate register for each building or area, with its address and notes. See at a glance how many assets each site has and how many need attention.

Quick capture

Pick the asset type from a list (with extinguisher types), add the location and asset ID, and choose its condition and service tag status. Save and go straight on to the next one.

Photos

Attach photos to each asset from the camera or your library. They're stored small and appear beside the asset in the report.

Search and filter

Find any asset by type, location, ID or note, and filter to just the defects or the overdue service tags.

PDF report

Share a clean A4 report of the site's register, with a summary, colour-coded conditions and photos, ready to email to a client or manager.

Spreadsheet export

Share the register as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, or copy it as text.

Works offline

Everything runs on your phone, so plant rooms, basements and car parks are no problem.

Backup and restore

Save everything, including photos, to one backup file, and restore it on a new phone.

Your data stays on your phone

Asset Capture has no account, no server and no tracking. Your registers and photos are stored on your device, and they only leave it when you share a report, a spreadsheet or a backup yourself.

  • No sign-up or login
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking
  • One-tap backup file you control

A record-keeping tool

Asset Capture records assets as you observe them on site. It does not inspect, test or certify anything, and its report is not a certificate of compliance or a statutory form.
