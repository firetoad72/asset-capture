Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026 · Applies to the Asset Capture app on iPhone

In short: everything you record in Asset Capture is stored on your device. The app has no account and no server. It sends nothing to us or anyone else, and it has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Who we are

Asset Capture is published by Michael Tabet, the developer listed on the App Store ("we", "us"). Asset Capture is an offline tool for recording fire safety assets and their condition.

What the app stores, and where

To do its job, the app stores what you enter, on your device only:

  • sites: their names, addresses and notes;
  • assets: their type, location, asset ID, condition, service tag status and notes;
  • photos you attach to assets.

You decide what to enter. If a note or photo includes personal information about someone, it stays on your device like everything else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your data to us or to any third party, and we cannot see or access it. There is no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo library access only when you choose to add a photo to an asset. Those photos are stored inside the app on your device.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it. Sharing a PDF report, a CSV spreadsheet or a backup, printing, or copying text, opens your device's share sheet or print dialog. You decide where it goes: email, Files, AirDrop, a printer, and so on. Those services handle the data under their own terms.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it, so keep your own backups of anything important.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security: its passcode, biometrics and encryption. Keep your device locked and up to date.

Children

Asset Capture is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before that happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us through the support page.
